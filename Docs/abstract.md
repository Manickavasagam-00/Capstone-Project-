# Abstract

The **Second-Hand Electronics Trading Platform** is a web-based marketplace designed to simplify the buying and selling of used electronic products. The platform provides a centralized environment where users can register, log in, list second-hand electronic products, browse available products, and manage their listings.

The system uses **Java Spring Boot** for backend development, **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** for the frontend, and **MySQL** for database management. The application aims to provide an organized and user-friendly platform for second-hand electronics trading while encouraging the reuse of electronic devices and reducing electronic waste.

The initial MVP focuses on user authentication, product listing, product management, and product browsing. Additional features such as ratings, reviews, messaging, online payments, and AI-based recommendations can be introduced as future enhancements.
