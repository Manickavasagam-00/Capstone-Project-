4. What problem are we solving?

Many people own electronic devices such as smartphones, laptops, tablets, cameras, and gaming consoles that they no longer use. Existing online marketplaces often contain fake listings, unclear product conditions, and limited verification, making it difficult for buyers to trust sellers.

The proposed platform provides a secure marketplace where verified users can buy and sell second-hand electronics. It improves transparency by allowing sellers to provide detailed product information, images, and condition descriptions while enabling buyers to search, compare, communicate, and review sellers before making a purchase.

8. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

A buyer can register and log in successfully.
A seller can create and manage product listings.
Buyers can search and filter products easily.
Orders can be placed successfully.
Buyers and sellers can communicate through the platform.
Users can submit ratings and reviews.
Admin can approve or reject listings.
Reports can be generated for platform management.
